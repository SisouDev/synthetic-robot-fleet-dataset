# Authorship

**Creator and author:** Maria Karolina Silva de Souza

Maria Karolina Silva de Souza is the creator and author of the synthetic dataset, its tabular metadata, explanatory documentation, relational diagram, and locally prepared publication package.

The official citation metadata is provided in [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff). File-level authorship and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in [`FILE_MANIFEST.json`](FILE_MANIFEST.json). Formats with native metadata support - including XLSX, PDF, SVG, and Jupyter Notebook - also carry embedded authorship metadata.

CSV has no portable embedded-author field that can be added without changing the table schema or breaking ordinary CSV readers. The CSV files are therefore attributed through this authorship statement, the data license, the citation file, and the author-attributed file manifest.
